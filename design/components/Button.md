An action or a link styled as one. Quiet is the default.

- `primary` (strait fill, `on-strait` text) at most once per view, for the thing the page is for: "Download CV", "Get in touch".
- `quiet` for secondary actions; `link` for an inline-looking action next to others.
- Pass `href` to render an `<a>`; otherwise it is a `<button>`.
- Verb first, sentence case, two or three words. No icons-only buttons.
