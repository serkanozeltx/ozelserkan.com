One entry in a publication list, cited in APA 7 style with the site owner's name in bold.

- Provide `authors` exactly as cited, `year`, `title`, `venue` (italicised), optional `details` (volume, issue, pages or city), `href` for the title, `doi` (without the prefix) and extra `links` ("PDF", "Slides").
- `self` is the string to bold; it defaults to "Özel, S.".
- `kind` is the eyebrow above the entry: "Journal article", "Book chapter", "Conference paper".
- Group entries by year, newest first, under an `h2` year heading. Entries are separated by a `hairline` rule.
- Never invent a DOI; leave it out if there isn't one.
